Projects

  1. May – Aug 2026

    Webtoon Originals Data Analysis and Statistical Inference

    • Python
    • Jupyter Notebook

    Ran Pearson correlation analysis on episode volume, platform tenure, views, likes, and subscribers. Views and likes showed moderate positive associations, while subscribers showed only weak correlation, suggesting subscriber growth is largely independent of structural factors alone.

    Applied Kruskal-Wallis and Dunn’s post-hoc test (Bonferroni-corrected) across four structural tiers. Differences were statistically significant with large effect sizes for every outcome, indicating that engagement follows a tiered pattern in which content quality can substitute for sheer episode volume.
